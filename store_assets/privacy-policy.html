Privacy Policy

App: Happy Kids Coloring  |  Developer: MinSoftware  |  Effective Date: November 20, 2026

MinSoftware built the Happy Kids Coloring app as a family-friendly coloring game for children. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from children or other users. Happy Kids Coloring does not require account registration, login, names, email addresses, location data, or access to your device contacts, camera, or microphone.

2. Local Storage

Your coloring progress, unlocked themes, and app preferences (such as sound settings) are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The current release of Happy Kids Coloring does not display third-party advertisements and does not collect analytics or tracking data.

4. In-App Purchases

If optional in-app purchases are offered, all payment transactions are processed securely by Google Play and are protected by a parental gate. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

5. Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is especially important to us. Because Happy Kids Coloring does not collect personal information from any user, it complies with applicable children's privacy regulations, including COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy.
